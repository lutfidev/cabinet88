DESIGN NOTES
A premium launcher, not a storefront: nothing on screen sells you anything. The CRT texture, marquee type and pixel sprites carry the nostalgia; layout, spacing and hit targets stay modern Android so the app is easy to use one-handed. Pixel art is deliberate placeholder art — swap in commissioned marquees per cabinet. Serpent 88 is genuinely playable (D-pad, swipe or arrow keys); the other nine share the same play shell so new cabinets need no new screens.
REVIEW SWITCHES
Three home directions and two cabinet-file layouts are wired to the Tweaks panel — Marquee / The Row / Playlist, and Poster / Tabbed. CRT scanlines toggle there too, and in the app's own settings.
