Privacy Policy for Cabinet88

Last updated 19 September 2026

Cabinet88 does not collect any data about you. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts and no network access. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because the app cannot transmit anything.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect or transmit personal information.
  • It does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not require an account, an email address or a sign-in.
  • It does not request any Android permission. The installed app declares none at all, including internet access.
  • It does not contain in-app purchases or subscriptions.

What is stored on your device

Cabinet88 saves a small amount of information on the phone itself, so the app remembers where you left off:

  • Your settings, such as whether CRT scanlines, sound, haptics and the high-contrast theme are on.
  • Your best score for each cabinet, and which cabinets you have played.
  • Which cabinet files you have opened, which is how trophies are awarded.

This is written to the app's own private storage using Android's standard preferences. It never leaves the device, it is not readable by other apps, and nobody — including the developer — can see it. Uninstalling Cabinet88 deletes all of it. There is nothing to request, export or erase from a server, because no server holds anything.

Leaderboards

The leaderboard shown inside a cabinet file is fictional sample content included with the app. It is not a real ranking, it is not shared with anyone, and no score of yours is uploaded to it.

Children

Cabinet88 is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Payment

Cabinet88 is a paid app, bought once on Google Play before it is downloaded. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, and the app itself never sees, requests or stores any payment information.

Changes to this policy
